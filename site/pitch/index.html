The instrument

a generative sequencer, and why prove one

A generative sequencer with a modular synth's oscillator in a $60 keyboard, with {{theorems}} theorems about the promises it makes.

a Eurorack case; middle row, a Mutable Instruments Braids reading CSAW, the oscillator this firmware compiles from source. Photo Paul Anthony, CC BY-SA 4.0

what it is

  • Four lanes, each a rhythm (Euclidean by default) choosing its own notes.
  • Nothing written down: the rules are the song.
  • Braids (Mutable Instruments), an FM voice and an 808 kit, in Lean.
  • All integers: the chip has no floating point.

why prove any of this

  • Promises you can hear: in key, on the beat, nothing stuck.
  • A test plays some settings; a theorem holds at all of them.
  • Fixed-point bugs are silent until they are noise or a crash.
  • No debugger on the chip: prove it before flashing.
  • The rhythm is a Christoffel word; the chord walks are a group.

What it does

four lanes · Braids, FM, an 808 kit, a filter · two sends · the PUNCH page · seven looks

the sequencer

  • Four lanes: hits, steps, speed, chance; the bar drawn as a circle.
  • FEEL: Euclid, rotated with accents, a cellular automaton, ratchets.
  • NOTES: random, walk, up, down, bounce, loop, Turing, step, Markov, grammar, chord.
  • HARMONY: key, scale lock, progressions as degrees, Tonnetz walks. Swing, MIDI in and out, external clock, arpeggiator, quantized keys.

the engines

  • Braids, all 48 shapes: analog, filter, voice, FM, physical, percussion, wavetable, noise.
  • Four-operator FM voice in Lean: piano, bell, bass, brass, glass, mallet, organ, clang.
  • 808 kit in Lean: kick, snare, clap, hats, toms, cowbell, cymbal.
  • Per-voice filter (low, band or high), opened by the envelope; in Lean, states proved in range.
  • Browser by engine or by type; per lane an envelope, an LFO, pan and level.

the effects

  • Clouds' reverb on a send: decay, brightness, return. Its tail is proved to die away.
  • Ping-pong delay on the same send: a note value at the song's tempo, feedback, tone.
  • Each lane sets its own send: one lane dry, another wet.

punch-in effects

  • REC makes the keys momentary effects on the whole mix, like the OP-XY's punch-in FX.
  • HALF and DOUBLE time, STUTTER, ROLL, octaves, SHIFT, GATE, CRUSH, WASH, THROW, BREAK, four mutes, four rolls.
  • Let go and it is gone: with no key held every effect is the identity, a theorem; the gate fades out in 1.5 ms.
1:20. Four pages. The sequencer: four lanes, each a rhythm and a note source, harmony on top, MIDI, an arpeggiator. The engines: Braids, all 48 shapes, the FM voice in Lean, an 808 kit in Lean, a per-voice filter. The effects: Clouds' reverb and a ping-pong delay on a send. The PUNCH page: the keys as momentary effects; letting go restores the mix, which is a theorem. Twenty-five seconds. The third slide to cut.
