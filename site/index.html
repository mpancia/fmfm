FORMALFRED  ·  M-VAVE FM-1  ·  firmware {{version}}

A generative sequencer for the FM-1, with its promises proved in Lean.

Four lanes each divide the clock into a rhythm and pick notes for it from a scale. The voice is Mutable Instruments' Braids with all 48 of its shapes, and a reverb and a ping-pong delay sit on a send. It ships with 128 patches. Alfred the butler bows at startup and waits in the corner whenever the clock stops.

The sequencer, the voices' arithmetic, the effects, the mix and the patch format are Lean 4, compiled to the chip. Lean's kernel checks {{theorems}} theorems about that code. The rest of the firmware is C, and the README lists it as trusted.

The simulator is the real firmware compiled to WebAssembly. Chrome or Edge for MIDI in.

What it does

Rhythm

Euclidean bars of 1 to 32 steps at eight clock divisions, or a rotated bar with accents, a cellular automaton, or ratchets. Each lane runs at its own speed, and where a lane stands in its bar depends only on how many clock ticks have passed, so the four cannot drift apart (run_clock_never_drifts).

Notes

Random, walk, up, down, bounce, loop, Turing, step, Markov, grammar or chord tones, inside a range and a scale. A chord progression can move the whole scale or hold a lane to the triad. Whatever the algorithm, every note a lane plays is in its scale (lane_note_in_scale).

Sound

199 sounds across Braids' analog, digital, physical, percussion, vowel, wavetable and noise engines, with envelope and timbre control, an LFO a lane, an arpeggiator on the keys, and the two sends.

The panel

Four buttons pick a group of pages; the same button again moves to the next page. The four knobs set the four values on screen.

Before you flash

  • Put the unit on the official V15 firmware first, and take a full-flash backup. Steps in docs/FM1.md.
  • The installer works over USB from Chrome or Edge and keeps the update path and the OCT- rescue.
  • Nothing here has been run on a physical FM-1. Use at your own risk.
