FORMALFRED

A generative sequencer for the M-VAVE FM-1, with its sequencing logic proved in Lean 4.

InstallFlash the latest release over USB from Chrome or Edge. Back up first. Try it in your browserThe real firmware, compiled to WebAssembly, with the screen, keys and knobs.

What it is

  • Four lanes, each a clock divider driving a Euclidean rhythm. A note algorithm (random, walk, up, down, bounce, loop) picks pitches inside a range, and a scale or chord adjusts them.
  • All 48 oscillator shapes of Mutable Instruments' Braids, 199 sounds, 122 patches, a preset browser, chord progressions and six looks.
  • Alfred, the butler. He bows at startup and waits in the corner when the clock stops.

What is proved

The lane logic is Lean, checked by Lean's kernel with only the standard axioms. Among the theorems: a bar with k hits plays exactly k notes, every note is in the chosen scale, a rest never plays, and no note is left sounding when its gate ends or the clock stops. The full list, with why each matters, is in docs/PROOFS.md. The voices, the screen and the C that calls the Lean code are not proved; the README lists what is trusted.

Before you flash

  • Put the unit on official V15 first, and take a full-flash backup. Steps in docs/FM1.md.
  • Nothing here has been tested on a physical FM-1 yet. Use at your own risk.
